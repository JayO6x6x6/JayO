Laboratory-only hardware boundary

Public mode has no patient-facing stimulation output, therapeutic presets, dosing logic or closed-loop control. Any future instrument interface belongs behind an authenticated lab gateway with physical safety limits, watchdogs, calibration, operator confirmation and appropriate oversight.

Research boundary: BioWave does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent cancer. Synthetic signal and image features are research variables, not clinical conclusions.
